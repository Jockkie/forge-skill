# The twelve disciplines in full

Each has a trigger, an action, an evidence requirement, and an anti-pattern. The evidence requirement applies at Full depth only. At Light depth the action still runs; only the written artefact is dropped.

Numbering matches `SKILL.md` §3: 1-5 spine, 6-9 gates, 10-12 output filters.

---

## Spine

### 1. Knowledge ingestion

**Trigger:** start of a cycle, or a subject not covered by the current pattern.
**Action:** read available sources until the marginal source adds nothing new. Saturation is the criterion, not a count. If you cannot reach saturation, say what remains unread.
**Evidence:** sources listed with title, origin, and the passage that mattered. Three is a floor for Full depth, not a target.
**Anti-pattern:** stopping at sources that confirm the synthesis you already had. Confirmation is not saturation.

### 2. Pattern compression

**Trigger:** after ingestion, before synthesis.
**Action:** compress to one sentence that stays true when every detail is removed. If it takes two, the compression is unfinished.
**Evidence:** the pattern sentence marked explicitly in the output.
**Anti-pattern:** a summary that enumerates instead of a pattern that holds. Test: remove a fact. If the sentence breaks, it was a summary.

### 3. Multi-perspective hold

**Trigger:** any choice with two or more legitimate options.
**Action:** hold at least three fundamentally different perspectives at once, drawn from the pool in `multi-agent.md`. Do not resolve early.
**Evidence:** each perspective labelled with its role, plus one line on where they disagree.
**Anti-pattern:** three restatements of one view. Test: could these three reach different conclusions? If not, regenerate.

### 4. Adversarial self-check (Melkor test)

**Trigger:** after synthesis, before choosing.
**Action:** read the synthesis as the person who most wants it to be wrong and has the standing to say so. Their strongest objection, not their easiest.
**Evidence:** the objection, and whether it broke the synthesis or not, with reason.
**Anti-pattern:** skipping the test because the synthesis feels good. Feeling good is when the test matters most.

### 5. Choice under uncertainty

**Trigger:** after 1-4.
**Action:** choose the option that fits the moment. Not the majority view, not the first option, not the most defensible one.
**Evidence:** chosen option, reason, rejected options, reason for rejection.
**Anti-pattern:** preference dressed as choice. Test: would you still choose this if you disliked it?

## Gates

### 6. Time horizon

**Trigger:** the decision has effect beyond a week.
**Action:** read the work in time. What was tried before and what happened. How this reads in six months. What delay costs.
**Evidence:** one line each for past, present, future.
**Anti-pattern:** treating the decision as new when the pattern has run before.

### 7. Source-of-truth ladder

**Trigger:** a factual claim is load-bearing.
**Action:** rank by authority for this specific claim, not by tier in general. A primary source beats a derivative. A recent authoritative source beats an older weaker one. An authority in an adjacent field is not an authority here.
**Evidence:** sources ranked, with the reason the top source is top.
**Anti-pattern:** accepting a source because it is generally respected. Also: attributing a method or idea to an organisation without a citation you can produce.

### 8. Failure catalog

**Trigger:** persistence is available and similar work has run before; or a cycle has just been shown to be wrong.
**Action:** read the catalog before starting. After a failure surfaces, append an entry linking the failure to the discipline that let it through.
**Evidence:** entry appended in the format in `state.md`.
**Anti-pattern:** writing to the catalog but never reading it. The read is the half that compounds.

### 9. Counterfactual rehearsal

**Trigger:** a decision whose outcome can later be observed.
**Action:** one line on the path not taken, and the signal that would show it was better.
**Evidence:** alternative, expected outcome, the signal to watch.
**Anti-pattern:** using it as regret. It is a calibration check, not a verdict.

## Output filters

### 10. Fit

**Trigger:** before anything leaves for the user.
**Action:** check against the pattern cache. What do they value, what do they avoid, what do they need to hear rather than want to hear.
**Evidence:** noted only when the check changed the output.
**Anti-pattern:** a technically correct synthesis that ignores the person receiving it. Also its inverse: softening a true finding to fit a preference.

### 11. Reasoning path

**Trigger:** end of cycle.
**Action:** show what was chosen and what was rejected. Three internal readings before delivery: as fact-checker, as voice-detector, as the reader who has to act on it. If the three disagree, the synthesis is not done.
**Evidence:** the path in the output, at the level set in `SKILL.md` §4.
**Anti-pattern:** the answer without the path, or the path in such volume that it buries the answer.

### 12. Accountability

**Trigger:** any output carrying a claim or a recommendation.
**Action:** state what was known, what was chosen, on what basis, with what confidence. Confidence is a signal, not a decoration: high means a primary source or direct verification, medium means inference from good sources, low means reasoning without grounding.
**Evidence:** source and confidence attached per claim.
**Anti-pattern:** uniform confidence across claims of unequal grounding.
